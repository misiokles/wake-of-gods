# War Machines on Map — Player Guide

Some treasures have uses far beyond their sale value. **War Machines on Map** adds adventure map graphics for four war machines and the Grail.

## What Can You Find?

- **Ballista** — a war machine that can support your army with ranged attacks.
- **First Aid Tent** — a support war machine used to heal creatures during battle.
- **Ammo Cart** — supplies ammunition for ranged troops.
- **Catapult** — a siege machine used in town assaults.
- **Grail** — a special artifact associated with building a Grail structure in a town.

The Ballista (Major), Ammo Cart and First Aid Tent (Minor) can enter the ordinary random artifact pool when the map permits them. The Catapult and the Grail can be placed on a map and picked up. The Catapult has the Special class. The Grail is a Relic and lacks an equipment slot, which keeps it outside the ordinary artifact pool.

## What About the Spell Book?

In **version 1.0.1**, this mod **no longer adds the ordinary Spell Book** to the pool of adventure-map artifacts. The earlier version allowed a hero to find a normal spellbook, but it did not teach any spells by itself.

For a book that grants spells on discovery, enable the separate **WoG Scripts: Spell Book** submod. Its magical book is an adventure-map *object*, not the normal spellbook artifact. It awards a selection of spells whose quality depends on the hero's Luck and Wisdom; a hero who needs a normal spellbook can also receive one.

## Compatibility

- This mod keeps the same internal identifier and the same five remaining artifact definitions.
- It does not replace or alter normal spellbook behavior in towns, starting hero equipment, scripted rewards, or other mods.
- Another mod may independently make the normal spellbook obtainable on the map.

*VCMI adaptation: version 1.0.3. In a native VCMI 1.8.0 test, all five could be picked up. An occupied Catapult slot prevented a second pickup. The ordinary artifact draw produced the Ballista, Ammo Cart and First Aid Tent over 6,000 draws. Full random map generation and client visuals are separate tests.*
