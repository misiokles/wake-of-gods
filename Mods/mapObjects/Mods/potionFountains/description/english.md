# Potion Fountains

Three enchanted fountains offer magical flasks that help in battle. Each visit gives a **single enchanted potion artifact with three battle charges**. The flask is equipped like a special hero artifact and disappears when its charges are exhausted.

## The three fountains

| Fountain | Potion | What it does | Charges |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fountain of Fortune | Hydromel Potion | **+2 Attack and +2 Defence** during combat | 3 battles |
| Valhalla's Fountain | Berserker Potion | **+10 Attack** and maximum Luck, but prevents spellcasting during combat | 3 battles |
| Venus's Fountain | Venus's Blessing | Maximum **Morale and Luck** during combat | 3 battles |

## Drinking and refilling

- The fountains **do not charge gold or other resources**.
- **Fountain of Fortune** also applies a temporary Luck result for the next battle: **−1, +1, +2 or +3 Luck**, with **25% chance each**.
- **Valhalla's Fountain** and **Venus's Fountain** have a weekly renewal: you can return for another potion once the fountain refreshes.
- The displayed story may mention filling *three flasks*, but the reward is actually **one potion artifact with three charges**, not three separate artifacts.
- Each potion occupies a hero miscellaneous artifact slot. The potion's benefits are active while it is equipped; after **three battles**, it disappears.

Berserker Potion's ban on spellcasting is a serious trade-off: use it on a hero who relies on physical force rather than magic.
