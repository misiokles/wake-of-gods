# Palace of Dreams

A mysterious palace of chance. Pay an entrance fee, open a magical chest and see whether your dream turns into a prize or disappointment.

## Playing the game

- Each play requires **at least 1,000 gold**.
- There are **five possible results**. The palace chooses a random eligible result; you cannot select a specific prize in advance.
- A completed visit is recorded. The palace refreshes its opportunities on the **next game day**.

## Possible outcomes

| Outcome | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Empty chest | Lose **1,000 gold** and receive nothing |
| Resource prize | Pay **1,000 gold** and receive **1–6** of a random precious resource (ore, mercury, gems, sulfur or crystal) |
| Treasure artifact | Pay **1,000 gold** and receive a random **Treasure-class artifact** |
| Minor artifact | Receive a random **Minor-class artifact** **and 2,000 gold** |
| Major artifact | Receive a random **Major-class artifact** **and 5,000 gold** |

**Important:** The last two results award gold in addition to the artifact, rather than deducting the advertised stake. This is how those results are currently configured, so the palace's potential jackpots are especially valuable.

Only one completed visit is allowed before the palace's next daily reset.
