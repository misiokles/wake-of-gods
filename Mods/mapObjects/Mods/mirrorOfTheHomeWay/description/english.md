# Mirror of the Home-Way

A magical mirror that can transport your hero back toward a town you control, using a powerful **Town Portal** effect.

## Service

- **Price: 1,000 gold** per successful use. You need at least that amount to accept the offer.
- The mirror casts **Town Portal at Expert mastery**; it does not permanently teach Town Portal or require the spell to be in your book.
- You can **decline** before paying.
- **Unlimited visits:** no daily or weekly visit limit is set. Return whenever you need to travel home and can afford the fee.
- The destination is handled by the Town Portal effect, so your options depend on your towns and the spell's targeting rules.

The mirror is especially helpful for returning an army to defend a threatened town or regroup after a long expedition.
