# Alms House

A modest refuge for heroes who are just beginning their adventures or have run short of supplies. The house offers help based on what your hero or kingdom is missing. **There is no admission fee.**

## What you can receive

- **+1 Attack** if your current Attack is 0.
- **+1 Defence** if your current Defence is 0.
- **+1 Spell Power** if your current Spell Power is below 2.
- **+1 Knowledge** if your current Knowledge is below 2.
- **1,000 experience** if your hero is below level 2.
- **1 wood, ore, mercury, sulfur, crystal or gem** when your kingdom has none of that resource.
- **1 gold** when your kingdom has no gold at all.

Multiple types of assistance can be awarded together when their conditions are met. They are gifts; you do not have to surrender artifacts or pay resources.

## Visiting

Each hero can claim assistance from a particular Alms House **once**. There is no daily or weekly renewal configured, so treat a house as a one-time stop for each hero. A hero who already received aid cannot repeatedly collect it from the same building.
