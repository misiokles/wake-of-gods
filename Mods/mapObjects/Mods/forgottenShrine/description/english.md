# Forgotten Shrine

*Long abandoned by its keepers, this weathered shrine still shelters an ancient spirit. Those who seek its blessing must offer their strength for the day, but the spirit may come to their aid when battle begins.*

## How it works

Visiting the **Forgotten Shrine** allows a hero to pray for **one randomly chosen blessing**. The chosen spell is prepared for the hero's **next battle** and is cast automatically at the beginning of that battle, at **Expert mastery**. Its configured duration is **50 combat rounds**, subject to the spell’s normal targeting, immunity and expiry rules. Frenzy, for example, ends on attack.

The shrine offers **20 possible spells**. Each blessing is chosen randomly; the shrine does not reveal which spell was selected before the player accepts the prayer.

| Visiting rule | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum movement | The hero must have **at least 201 movement points** remaining. |
| Prayer cost | **All remaining movement points** are consumed. |
| Choice | The hero may **decline** the prayer without taking the blessing. |
| Reward | One random Expert-level spell prepared for the **next battle**. |
| Duration | **50 rounds** of that battle, subject to normal spell behavior. |
| Repeat visits | A hero cannot obtain another shrine blessing **while one remains active**, even at a different shrine. Once that bonus has expired, the shrine can be used again. |

## Possible blessings

**Protection and survival:** Shield, Air Shield, Fire Shield, Protection from Air, Protection from Fire, Protection from Water, Protection from Earth, Anti-Magic, Stone Skin, and Magic Mirror.

**Attack and battlefield advantage:** Bless, Bloodlust, Precision, Prayer, Mirth, Fortune, Haste, Slayer, Frenzy, and Counterstrike.

The spell is cast as a passive opening spell for the army after tactics and before the first round; spell-level restrictions (such as the Orb of Inhibition or Recanter’s Cloak) may prevent casting altogether. **a particular creature's immunity or the spell's targeting rules may prevent it from benefiting**. In particular, do not assume that every special spell affects every troop in exactly the same way.

## Advice for adventurers

The shrine is useful when preparing for a difficult encounter: its blessing is saved for the next battle, but the prayer leaves the hero unable to move farther that day. If you have 200 movement points or fewer, you will need to return when you have more strength. You do not need to know the chosen spell or have it in your spellbook to receive the shrine's battle-start effect.

A blessing is **not a permanent spell learned by the hero**. Nor does the shrine award gold, resources, artifacts, or experience.
